Out of the garage in four steps.

  1. Install

    A desktop app for Windows. Public download coming soon.

  2. Add your keys

    Onboarding asks which providers you use and builds your model chain. Keys stay on your PC in a local .env file.

  3. Connect the game

What does it cost?

No app subscription. The AI is your choice: Ollama Cloud has a free tier, local Ollama needs no key, and paid providers bill you directly for what you use.

Which AI do I need?

Any one of Ollama Cloud, Groq, Google Gemini, OpenRouter, Anthropic, an OpenAI-compatible endpoint, or Ollama on your PC. Add more to build a chain with fallback.

Where do my keys go?

Into a local .env file on your PC. Race context is sent only to the providers in your chain.

What runs on my PC?

The telemetry analysis, translation, Piper speech and, if you pick it, faster-whisper speech recognition. The phrasing is done by the model you choose, which can also be Ollama on your PC.

Which games work?

Assetto Corsa Competizione, through shared memory and the optional Broadcasting API. F1 25, through its UDP telemetry.

Can I see it on my phone?

Yes. The dashboard works in a phone browser over your LAN.

How good are the answers?

In our internal tests, accuracy on a 320-scenario suite went from 76% to 93–94% once the app started doing the analysis itself. Results depend on the model you choose.

Is it official?

No. airaceGP is an unofficial fan-made tool, not affiliated with the makers of either game.

Credits · standing on other people's work

airaceGP is a small project built on a lot of open work. These are some of the people whose code, voices, translations and track maps make it possible. Full credits and notices are kept with the app.

Voice

Piper by Michael Hansen and the Rhasspy project, and piper-plus by ayutaz. Voice datasets by Thorsten Mueller (German), Carlos Fonseca (Spanish), czyzi0 (Polish), the Nabu Casa voice-datasets team (Portuguese, Czech), the SIWIS project (French).

Speech recognition

Vosk by Alpha Cephei for the wake word. faster-whisper by SYSTRAN, riding on CTranslate2 by OpenNMT and SYSTRAN, with weights from OpenAI's Whisper. All MIT or Apache 2.0.

Translation
